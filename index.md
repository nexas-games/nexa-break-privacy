# Privacy Policy for nexa-break

**Last updated: 10 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how nexa-break ("the App", "we", "us") handles
information when you use our mobile game.

## Summary

nexa-break does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.
The game runs entirely on your device. Nothing you do in the game is sent
to us or to any third party.

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, request, or have access to:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
- Your location (precise or approximate)
- Your device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, MAC address)
- Your photos, files, contacts, calendar, or any other personal data
- Your usage analytics, crash reports, or gameplay statistics

The App does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or any
third-party SDKs that collect user data.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The App saves your game progress (unlocked levels, high scores, in-game
coin balance) locally on your device using Android's app-private storage.
This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not accessible to us or to any third party
- Is deleted automatically when you uninstall the App
- Can be cleared at any time via Android Settings → Apps → nexa-break →
  Storage → Clear data

## Permissions

nexa-break requests no Android permissions. It has no access to the
internet, camera, microphone, location, storage, contacts, or any other
sensitive device feature.

## Children's Privacy

nexa-break does not collect any information from anyone, including
children under the age of 13. The App is safe for users of all ages.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services that collect
user data.

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect certain
information about your use of the Play Store in accordance with Google's
own Privacy Policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
This is outside our control and unrelated to the App itself.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version
will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued
use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
